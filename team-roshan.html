Biography

The Vision Behind the Cut

Roshan's path into hairdressing began on fashion sets, assisting on photoshoots where he learned that a great cut has to hold its shape under studio lights, camera angles, and real life alike. That editorial grounding still shapes every haircut he gives today at The Color Bar.

With over a decade behind the chair, Roshan leads The Color Bar's cutting team, known for precision dry-cutting, modern shag and bob silhouettes, and a belief that a great haircut should work with — not against — a client's natural texture and lifestyle.

Roshan also mentors The Color Bar's junior stylist program, coaching the next generation on precision, consultation, and the confidence to cut boldly.
