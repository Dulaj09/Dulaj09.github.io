About Us

About The Color Bar

Tucked along Lighthouse Street in the heart of Galle, The Color Bar was created for those who see beauty as a form of self-expression, not just an errand. We bring together modern technique, premium products, and genuinely personal care to help every client leave feeling like the best version of themselves.

Our team of experienced stylists stays current with the latest colour, cutting, and treatment trends from around the world, while our studio is designed as a calm, moody retreat from the everyday. Whether you're here for a subtle refresh or a complete transformation, you're in trusted hands.
