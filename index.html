Our Story

Built on one belief: your hair should feel entirely your own

How We Began

A colour bar, in the most literal sense

The Color Bar opened its doors on Lighthouse Street with a simple idea: a salon where every colour formula is mixed at a dedicated bar, in full view, tailored to the person sitting in the chair — not pulled from a box.

What started as a single colourist's chair has grown into a full studio of cutting, treatment, and bridal specialists, all working from that same original idea: nothing generic, everything considered.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Two ideas that guide every appointment at The Color Bar.

Our Mission

To give every guest hair that suits their real life — colour that grows out gracefully, cuts that hold their shape, and treatments that leave hair genuinely healthier than when they walked in.

Our Vision

To be the studio Sri Lankans and visitors alike think of first when they want hair that could sit comfortably in any city in the world — without leaving Galle.

Our Philosophy

Luxury is not loud

We believe true luxury is quiet: a room that isn't rushed, a colourist who listens before mixing anything, and results that look effortless because the process wasn't.
