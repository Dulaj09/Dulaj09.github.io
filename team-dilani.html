Biography

The Artist Behind the Colour

Dilani discovered her love for colour theory while working alongside a photography studio, where she became fascinated by how light and tone could completely reshape a portrait. That curiosity led her to formal colour training, and eventually to The Color Bar, where she has spent the past eight years perfecting her craft.

Known for meticulous balayage, seamless colour correction, and a discerning eye for tones that flatter every skin type, Dilani approaches each client as a unique canvas. She regularly trains with international colour houses to stay ahead of global trends and finds particular joy in dramatic, confidence-building transformations.

Off the clock, Dilani mentors junior colourists at The Color Bar's in-house training sessions, passing on the technique and patience that colour artistry demands.
